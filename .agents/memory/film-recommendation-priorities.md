---
name: Film recommendation priorities
description: Product requirements for Film Finder's first-run setup and recommendation relevance.
---

New users choose their streaming services before seeing recommendations. Suggestions must be available on at least one selected service and should closely reflect watched titles while favoring high viewer ratings.

**Why:** The user explicitly wants platforms selected at the start, a strict availability filter, stronger similarity to watched films, and high ratings.

**How to apply:** Preserve platform choice as a hard eligibility filter and watched films as a core relevance signal when changing onboarding or recommendation ranking.
