---
name: Film recommendation priorities
description: Product requirements for Film Finder's first-run setup and recommendation relevance.
---

New users choose their streaming services before seeing recommendations. Suggestions must be available on at least one selected service and should closely reflect watched titles, including shared actors or directors where possible, while favoring high viewer ratings. Film cards should show the director and principal cast instead of a plot synopsis.

**Why:** The user explicitly wants platforms selected at the start, a strict availability filter, recommendations connected to watched films through cast or directing, high ratings, and director/cast information on each film card.

**How to apply:** Preserve platform choice as a hard eligibility filter and watched films, shared actors, and shared directors as core relevance signals when changing recommendation ranking; avoid replacing the card's director/cast information with plot summaries.
